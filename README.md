# Football Team Management App

## Description
This is a dynamic football team management web application that allows users to build, manage, and modify their custom football teams. The app supports adding players, dynamically positioning them based on tactical formations, calculating team chemistry, and more. It also allows for saving and loading data, ensuring an interactive and engaging experience for users.

## Features
- **Dynamic Player Management**: Add, modify, and remove players with details such as name, position, and stats.
- **Formation Selection**: Choose and apply different formations (e.g., 4-3-3, 4-4-2) to organize the team.
- **Chemistry Calculation**: Automatically calculates and displays the chemistry score based on player links (club, league, nationality).
- **LocalStorage**: Save the team configuration for later use and load it automatically.
- **Responsive Design**: The app adapts to different screen sizes for desktop, tablet, and mobile users.
- **Drag-and-Drop**: Drag and drop players to modify positions or make substitutions.
- **Formation Validation**: Ensures that player positions are valid according to the selected formation.

## Technologies Used
- **HTML**: For the structure of the web application.
- **CSS (Tailwind CSS)**: For styling the UI in a responsive and modern way.
- **JavaScript**: For dynamic behavior, such as adding/removing players, changing formations, and calculating team chemistry.
- **LocalStorage**: For saving and retrieving user data locally.

## How to Use
1. **Create a Team**: Use the form to add players to your team, providing their name, position, and stats.
2. **Choose a Formation**: Select a formation from the available options (e.g., 4-3-3, 4-4-2), and the players will automatically be positioned according to the formation.
3. **Modify Players**: You can drag and drop players to change their positions or substitute them.
4. **View Chemistry**: The app will display the total chemistry score based on player connections.
5. **Save and Load**: Your team and formation will be saved automatically, and you can load it again on your next visit.
